## Overview

Green synthesis of nanoparticles using plant extracts has emerged
as an eco-friendly and sustainable alternative to conventional
chemical fabrication methods. This study reports the synthesis of
copper nanoparticles (CuNPs) using polyherbal extracts of
*Hygrophila auriculata* and *Leucas aspera* — two plants with
significant ethnomedicinal importance in traditional Indian
medicine systems. Plant phytochemicals act as both reducing and
capping agents, eliminating the need for toxic chemical reagents.

The synthesised CuNPs were comprehensively characterised and
evaluated for their biomedical potential including cytotoxicity
against cancer cell lines, antioxidant capacity, and antibacterial
activity against healthcare-associated pathogens.

## Objectives

- Synthesise copper nanoparticles via green/biogenic route using
  polyherbal plant extracts
- Characterise CuNPs using spectroscopic and microscopic techniques
- Evaluate cytotoxic potential against cancer cell lines
- Assess antioxidant capacity using DPPH radical scavenging assay
- Determine antibacterial activity against healthcare-associated
  pathogens

## Methodology

### Nanoparticle Synthesis
| Step | Details |
|------|---------|
| Plant material | Fresh leaves of *H. auriculata* and *L. aspera* |
| Extraction | Aqueous polyherbal extract preparation |
| Synthesis | Biogenic reduction of copper salt using plant extract |
| Stabilisation | Phytochemical capping agents |

### Characterisation Techniques
| Technique | Purpose | Key Information |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| FTIR | Functional group identification | Confirms phytochemical capping of CuNPs |
| XRD | Crystal structure analysis | Phase confirmation, crystallite size |
| FESEM | Surface morphology | Nanoparticle size and shape distribution |
| EDAX | Elemental composition | Confirms copper as major element |
| TLC | Extract compound separation | Phytochemical profile of plant extract |

### Phytochemical Analysis
| Technique | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Qualitative screening | Identify secondary metabolites present |
| GC-MS | Identify and quantify phytochemical compounds |

### Biological Activity Assays
| Assay | Target | Method |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Cytotoxicity | Cancer cell lines | MTT / cell viability assay |
| Antioxidant | Free radical scavenging | DPPH radical assay |
| Antibacterial | Healthcare pathogens | Zone of inhibition / MIC |
